About Me

Hello! I am Büşra Başoğlu, a senior-year student in the Department of Geomatics Engineering at Hacettepe University. Throughout my undergraduate education, I have developed a deep passion for transforming complex spatial data into meaningful, accessible, and interactive digital experiences.

My core academic and technical interests lie in Web GIS, spatial database management, remote sensing analysis, and disaster management mapping. I enjoy bridging the gap between traditional cartography and modern web technologies, enabling users to explore geographic information dynamically.

This website serves as my personal digital portfolio and map journal for the GMT 458: Web GIS course, where I showcase custom web mapping applications built using HTML5, CSS3, Leaflet, and OpenLayers.

Technical Skills & Engineering Focus

  • Web Mapping & GIS: Building interactive vector layers, marker clustering, and custom spatial bounding using Leaflet & OpenLayers.
  • Spatial Analysis Software: Active utilization of QGIS, Netcad, and Google Earth Engine for environmental and regional mapping projects.
  • Remote Sensing: Processing satellite imagery and assessing environmental changes (such as wildfire severity indices).
  • Version Control: Managing collaborative and individual engineering projects via Git and GitHub workflows.
